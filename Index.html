How Everything Works
🏆 Goal of the Game

You start with a bankroll and nothing else. Everything you do — sit down at a poker table, travel to a new city, buy a house, take out a loan, spend a night out — moves money in or out of that one bankroll number. There's no fixed "win" screen: the goal is to grow your bankroll and the empire it buys (houses, businesses, corporations, investments, possessions) into as high a Reputation Score as you can, for as long as you want to keep playing, while managing two hidden costs along the way — your Happiness & Energy (burn out and you're forced to stop) and your real bankroll risk (bust it completely and it's game over). Optionally, set a target bankroll and a pace on Home and the game will project when you'll get there. Everything below explains exactly how each system feeds into that.

A complete rundown of every screen, button and system in the game. Tap a heading to expand it.

♠ Play & the Table

Opens "Choose your table" — pick a game type and player count for the city you're currently in, then a buy-in slider sets how many chips you sit down with (capped by your bankroll). Games in other cities are hidden until you travel there.

This is a self-contained 8-max No-Limit Hold'em simulator: the deck, hand evaluator and side-pot math all run in the page itself. Seven bots fill the table, each playing a distinct, fixed style that's kept hidden until the end of the session (or turned on in Settings) — part of the game is reading who's who from how they play.

Table toolbar icons (top of the table screen):

  • Table strength / Game type / Players dropdowns — change the bots' skill level or the format without leaving the table (resets it).
  • Home — back to the Home screen (bankroll, graph, goals).
  • SH / MY / LN / VT — quick jumps to Shop, My House, Loans and Vault without quitting the table.
  • Settings (gear) — sound, auto-deal, instant mode, card size and more; see the Settings section below.
  • Z (Zoom) — hides the extras and enlarges the table for easier viewing on a small screen.
  • RS (Range stop) — see the Range Stop section below.
  • PO (Pot odds) — see Pot Odds & Outs below.
  • H (Hand history) — replays every hand you've played this session.
  • HR (Hand Reading) — the full Hand Reading Trainer; see below.
  • ? (Hand reading quiz) — a lighter in-the-moment prompt to funnel an opponent's range from their action and the board.
  • i (info) — this table's stakes, rake and bot-style details.

If your stack at the table runs out mid-session, a "Top up chips" button lets you rebuy more from your bankroll without leaving your seat.

Ending a session (the quit button) stops the current hand, folds your stack back into your bankroll, and generates a Session report you can download as a PDF, a full JSON history, or a PokerStars/HM-format hand history file. If you've played through to the end of a day, an Expenses screen bills that night's hotel (or waives it if you own a house there), food, local travel and entertainment straight from your bankroll before you can head back Home.

🎰 Online Casino

Reached from the "Choose your table" screen. Three quick side wagers against your bankroll, separate from the poker table itself — all negative expected value by design, so treat them as flavor, not strategy:

  • Coin Toss — pick a % of your bankroll to wager; 45% chance to double it, 55% chance to lose it.
  • Jackpot — pick a % of your bankroll to wager; a 1-in-40 (2.5%) chance to win 35× your wager, otherwise you lose it.
  • Lottery — $1 a ticket, buy as many as you like; each ticket is an independent 1-in-13,000,000 shot at a $1,000,000 top prize.
✈️ Travel

Moves you between the game's poker cities (and, separately, to rejuvenation-getaway cities that have no poker table at all — just entertainment to restore Happiness/Energy). Flights and visas (where needed, one-time per city) are billed immediately from your bankroll on confirm — it's free to travel back to a city you're already in or have already visaed. You can only sit down at tables in whichever city you're currently in.

🛍 Shop

Where your winnings turn into an empire, split into tabs:

  • Houses — buy in any city you've visited (three tiers each); owning one waives that city's hotel bill and can be leased out for passive daily rent when you're not there.
  • Businesses — storefronts (restaurants, bars, coffee/book shops, taxi/scooter rentals, a crypto fintech startup) that you can upgrade through 5 levels each for bigger daily income.
  • Corporations — whole companies bought outright for cash; the single biggest lever for Reputation and net worth in the game.
  • Investments — stocks, options, commodities, crypto spot/futures, a steady crypto yield app, a Hot Web3 Projects launchpad (with a one-time Launch Token founder action per project), and a Fixed Deposit that compounds daily and can never lose value.
  • Cars, yachts, private jets, clothes, jewelry & furniture — cosmetic and lifestyle possessions that add to your net worth and show up in My House. Yachts (Marina) and private jets (Hangar) range from an entry-level sportboat or single-engine jet all the way up to a full custom megayacht or VIP-airliner conversion.

Every purchase debits your bankroll through the same transaction ledger as everything else, so it's visible in your P/L and transaction history. Everything you own here also feeds your Reputation score.

🎭 Entertainment

A per-city catalog of things to spend bankroll on any time — arcades, tours, beach days, fine dining and more. Each purchase moves your Happiness and Energy meters (relaxing outings restore them, higher-effort ones cost a little), and every purchase is logged in your P/L like any other transaction. Unlike Shop items, these are never "owned" — you can repeat the same activity any time you can afford it.

My House & Possessions

Lists everything you own outside of a pure cash sense — a card per city where you hold property (with any furniture placed inside), your wardrobe, your jewelry box, and your garage of owned cars — plus a combined possessions net worth. Houses you own but aren't living in can be leased out from here for daily rental income.

🏦 Loans

Borrow against an owned house, business, or investment portfolio when your bankroll's short of a purchase, then repay in full or in part whenever you like. Outstanding balances accrue interest, so a loan left running costs more the longer it's open — and letting one run unpaid risks foreclosure on whatever secured it.

🔒 Vault

A safe place to set cash aside. Money in the Vault is protected from buy-ins, Shop spending and Casino wagers until you deliberately move it back — useful for locking in a win. It earns no interest; it just stays put.

Hand History

A replay browser for every hand you've played this session — step through each action street by street, skip preflop-fold hands with the "Played hands only" filter, or copy a hand's full action out to your clipboard to study or share later.

Hand Reading Trainer

Every hand you've played this session loads automatically. You can also import a downloaded hand history .txt file — yours from a past session, or someone else's — to study it too. For each street it walks through what an opponent's range looks like and how their action narrows it: a simplified teaching funnel, not a mind-reader or solver, meant to build the habit of asking "what does this range look like now?"

Range Stop

A discipline tool at the table. Check off exactly which starting hands you're willing to play on a 169-hand grid (with quick presets like "top %", "A2s+" or "Pairs"). While it's enabled, any preflop hand that isn't checked is auto-folded for you the instant it's dealt — hands you've already committed to later in the same hand won't be yanked back.

A built-in "Compute min BB" tool simulates 3,600 hands of your checked range against a chosen mix of opponent styles using a simplified tight-passive line, then reports a win rate and the minimum stack depth your range needs to hold up statistically.

Pot Odds & Outs

A live calculator for whatever spot you're currently in at the table: how many outs actually improve your hand (not ones that merely pair the board and help everyone equally), the price you're being asked to call relative to the pot, and the resulting math for whether calling is profitable. A learning tool, not a guarantee — an opponent can still hold something bigger even when the numbers favor you.

Happiness & Energy

Two 0-100 meters shown on Home. Entertainment purchases move them; every trip day on the road drains both a little (offset by that night's hotel tier or your own house), and every 200 lifetime hands played knocks both down further from grind fatigue. If either bottoms out at 0 you'll hit a burnout event, so it pays to spend on Entertainment or take a Relax day now and then.

Reputation Score

A single 0-100 number summarizing your empire, blended from four weighted pillars: Houses owned (25 pts), Businesses owned and upgraded (20 pts), Corporations owned (20 pts), and Bankroll relative to the cost of the entire empire (35 pts). 100 means you own everything, maxed out, with the cash to prove it — this is the closest thing the game has to an overall "score."

Your Pace & Goals

Set an average hands-per-day and a target bankroll on the Home screen and it'll project roughly how long you're on track to reach that goal, based on your actual results so far.

Bankroll & Profit/Loss

Every buy-in, cash-out, hotel/travel bill and shop transaction is grouped from your bankroll ledger into a daily or monthly Profit & Loss view on Home, alongside your career graph and recent sessions. The full transaction log is also viewable and clearable from Settings.

⚙ Settings
  • Player name — shown at the table and on session reports.
  • Bankroll — your persistent balance, fixed at the difficulty (Easy/Medium/Hard) you chose at the start; view it in any currency (INR/GBP/USD), with a viewable/clearable transaction history.
  • Sound / Game sounds — master audio switch and table sound effects.
  • Automatic deal — deals the next hand on its own a few seconds after one ends.
  • Instant mode — grinds through volume fast: folds deal instantly, even showdown wins barely pause.
  • Vibration — buzzes when it's your turn and when a hand ends (device-dependent).
  • Reveal player styles — shows each bot's style (LAG/TAG/Passive/Pro) live instead of only at session end.
  • Freeroll collection — when on, 1 big blind is skimmed from any pot of 10bb+ (on top of house rake) toward a running freeroll total shown on Home.
  • Your card size — resizes just your own hole cards at the table.
  • Gameplay strength — makes bots at every venue play weaker or stronger on top of the venue's own difficulty; resets the table.
  • Reset all data — permanently wipes everything stored in this browser (bankroll, history, session state, name, preferences). Cannot be undone.
⚠️ Going Bankrupt

If your off-table bankroll hits zero with nothing left in play anywhere either, you'll see a dedicated bust screen with money-management pointers (keep 20-30 buy-ins in reserve, don't play with money you need, move down in stakes before you're forced to) rather than the game simply stopping — a nudge to reset and start over with a plan.

🎰 Casino

Three ways to put your bankroll on the felt. Pick a game.

🎰 Coin Toss

One toss. Pick what percentage of your bankroll you're willing to put on the felt, then hit Toss. 45% of the time it doubles; 55% of the time you lose the whole wager.

Win chance is fixed at 45% every toss, independent of the percentage you choose.

💰 Jackpot

One pull. Pick what percentage of your bankroll you're willing to put on the felt, then hit Pull. 1 in 40 pulls hits the jackpot and pays 35× your wager; the other 39 in 40, you lose the whole wager.

Win chance is fixed at 1-in-40 (2.5%) every pull, independent of the percentage you choose. Expected return is well below break-even — this is a long-shot side game, not a strategy.

🎟 Lottery

$1 a ticket. Buy as many as you like, then hit Buy Tickets. Each ticket is a separate 1-in-13,000,000 shot at the $1,000,000 top prize — if any of your tickets hits, you win the top prize once.

Odds are fixed at 1-in-13,000,000 per ticket. Buying more tickets raises your total chance but each ticket is still a huge long shot.

Pot Odds & Outs
⚠️ You've gone bust

Your bankroll just hit zero — nothing left in your balance, and nothing left on the table either. It happens to every player who plays long enough. What matters now is how you respond to it.

Money management
  • Keep at least 20–30 buy-ins in reserve for whatever stakes you play — dropping below that is what turns ordinary variance into a bust.
  • Keep poker money and living-expense money completely separate. Never play with money you actually need.
  • Move down in stakes as soon as your bankroll shrinks — don't wait until it's gone to react.
  • Set a stop-loss before you sit down (e.g. 3 buy-ins for a session) and actually leave when you hit it.
  • Log every session, win or lose. You can't manage a risk you're not measuring.
Tilt management
  • Learn your own early warning signs — playing hands you'd normally fold, bigger bet sizes out of frustration, wanting action just to "feel something."
  • Take a mandatory break after any big loss. Step away before your next decision, not after it.
  • Never try to win it back in the same session — that instinct is how one bad beat turns into a bust.
  • Judge yourself on decisions, not results. A good decision can still lose a hand; a bad one can still win.
  • If tilt keeps costing you money, treat it as a leak like any other — it's fixable with the same deliberate work you'd put into your ranges.

This clears your bankroll, possessions and session history, and starts a brand-new career from scratch.
