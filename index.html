Kenji's Poker Challenge

A practice table that plays for keeps. NLHE, PLO5, or Mixed cash games — and a real WSOP $10,000 Main Event tournament — against seven bots, each with a hidden style. Real study tools built into every hand. And a whole life outside the felt — bankroll, empire, and burnout — that rises or falls with how well you play.

At the table

Play sharper, not just longer

The table is a self-contained simulator — deck, hand evaluator, and side-pot math all run in the page, across No-Limit Hold'em, Pot-Limit Omaha, Mixed cash games, and a real tournament. The study tools sit right alongside it, not in a separate app.

8mx
Eight-Max Cash Games
No-Limit Hold'em, Pot-Limit Omaha (5-card), or Mixed — alternating one NLHE hand with one PLO5 hand. Seven bots fill the table, each playing a distinct, fixed style — TAG, LAG, Passive, or Fish — kept hidden until you end the session, or revealed live from Settings.
WSOP
The WSOP $10,000 Main Event
A real tournament, not just a cash table: TAG, Passive, and Pro bots only, a 60,000-chip starting stack, escalating blind levels with a button-posted ante, live standings, and the Main Event's own real payout ladder. Bust out and it's over — no rebuys, no reloads.
HR
Hand Reading Trainer
A street-by-street range funnel for every hand you play — this session, or one you've imported from elsewhere.
PO
Pot Odds & Outs
Live math for whatever spot you're in: which outs actually help you, the price you're being asked to call, and whether calling is profitable.
RS
Range Stop
Check off exactly which starting hands you're willing to play on a 169-hand grid. Anything outside it folds itself, the instant it's dealt.
H
Hand History
Replay any hand from this session, and export the whole thing as a PDF report, a full JSON history, or a PokerStars/HM-format file.
FR
Freeroll Collection
Toggle it on and 1 big blind gets skimmed from every pot of 10bb or more, on top of the house rake, building a running freeroll total shown on Home.
TBL
Table Controls
Dial the bots' skill level, the game type, and the player count without leaving your seat.
Your empire

One bankroll — everything hangs off it

Buy-ins, hotel bills, a new house, a bad loan. It's all one number, moving in and out. Grow it into an empire — and a Reputation Score — that means something outside the hand you're playing.

BK
Bankroll & P&L
Every transaction rolls into a career graph and a daily or monthly profit-and-loss view, backed by a full, clearable ledger in Settings.
TRV
Travel
Move between real poker cities, or getaway cities with no table at all — just rest, to bring your Happiness and Energy back up.
SH
Shop
Houses, upgradable businesses, corporations, and investments — stocks, options, commodities, crypto, a launchpad, fixed deposits — plus cars, yachts, jets, and the rest.
LN·VT
Loans & Vault
Borrow against what you already own, or move winnings somewhere the table can't reach.
REP
Reputation Score
The real scoreboard — your houses, businesses, corporations, and bankroll, tracked together as one number.
GL
Pace & Goals
Set a target bankroll and a pace, and the game projects when you'll actually get there.
Off the felt

The grind wears on — so step away sometimes

A small row of tools that have nothing to do with your bankroll. For the breaks between sessions.

DR
Doctor
General symptom info for travelling players. Emergency use only — never a substitute for real medical care.
NU
Nutrition
A vegan/Jain daily nutrition chart, plus a natural travel food guide with swaps for eating well on the road.
EX
Exercise
Desk stretches and movement breaks, built for a body that's been sitting at the table for hours.
SU
Sudoku
A quick, timed puzzle break, with a top-10 best-times board to chase.
K♦
Poker Rooms & Tournament Series
A world directory of real, known poker rooms with their typical stakes, plus the major live tournament series that travel to them.
♞
Chess
A full game against a strong bot, with adjustable time control and search depth.
★
Moon & Nakshatra
Sidereal moon phase and nakshatra, calculated locally, with the next new and full moon — plus a lookup for any of the 27 nakshatras and when the moon reaches it.
BR
Breathing
Relaxation and heart-rate-calming techniques, including pranayama, plus a guided pacer.
DY
Diary
A private, password-encrypted notebook, kept separate from everything else in the game.

Everything above — the deck, the side pots, even the moon math — runs locally in your browser. Your bankroll never leaves your device.
